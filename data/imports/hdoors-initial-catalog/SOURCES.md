## Evidence and normalization policy

- `catalog.json` is the authoritative review dataset. Each record carries source URL/date/ID, field-level evidence notes, unknowns, conflicts, generated fields, price observation, and media permission state.
- Retail prices are dated observations only. They are not imported as Hdoors selling prices.
- Explicit door-leaf dimensions are normalized to millimetres. Ambiguous retailer dimensions and manufacturer ranges remain in `sourceConfiguration` and are not placed into leaf/unit dimension columns.
- Only one documented configuration is represented per model. Published size lists were not expanded combinatorially.
- Every imported product is `DRAFT`; every variant is `INQUIRY`, has `price_amount = NULL`, and has zero unconfirmed stock. The required legacy product price is the application's established non-purchasable compatibility sentinel `0`; no active variant uses it.
- No third-party image is downloaded, hotlinked, or uploaded. Reuse permission is `UNKNOWN` for every record, so drafts have no imported image.

## Important review flags

- ProfilDoors 5FN page heading/URL says 5FN, while its characteristics block displayed model 4 on retrieval. Publication must wait for manufacturer confirmation.
- Jaway JW-101 displays `2050×96` while its URL says `2050×960`; dimensions are not normalized.
- Several Jaway measurements do not label leaf versus complete-unit basis, so they remain source text.
- PRIX Zadoor Venice shows equivalent centimetre/millimetre values but does not label dimension semantics; it remains a legacy inquiry configuration.

## Generated by AI

Internal SKUs, localized slugs, Armenian/English/Russian titles and concise descriptions, and the selection of one source-listed OPPA size were generated. No unsupported performance, warranty, certification, package, stock, or outdoor-suitability claim was added.
